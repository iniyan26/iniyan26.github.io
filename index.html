About

I'm Iniyan, a B.Tech graduate in Electronics and Communication Engineering from SRM Institute of Science and Technology. I work in KiCad on schematic capture and PCB layout, and I have hands-on experience with STM32 board design, USB-C power principles, CAN bus communication and electronic troubleshooting.

My projects focus on the parts every board needs: clean power input, voltage regulation, protection, and a layout that passes its design rule check. I'm also training in Altium Designer and co-authored an IEEE conference paper on CAN bus monitoring.
